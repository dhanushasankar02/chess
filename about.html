♞ HISTORICAL TIMELINE

28 Years of Innovation & Excellence

Key milestones that defined our trajectory from a local Swiss club to a world-famous academy.

1998

Academy Founded in Zurich

GM Alexander Volkov establishes Apex Chess Academy with a focus on classical endgame mastery and candidate move calculation.

2008

FIDE International Accreditation

Certified as an official Senior FIDE Training Center, launching our international youth prodigy grant program.

2018

Stockfish Neural Engine Integration

Pioneered cloud automated PGN game analysis, providing instant candidate move diagnostics for over 10,000 active students.

2026

Global Hybrid Masterclass Expansion

Operating physical centers in Zurich & Prague alongside a global virtual masterclass platform serving students in 40+ nations.
