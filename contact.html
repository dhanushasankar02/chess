♞ KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Support Questions

Everything you need to know about batch enrollments, PGN engine audits, FIDE ratings, and GM mentorship.

GAME AUDITS

How do I submit my PGN games for Stockfish 16 audit?

You can paste your Lichess, Chess.com, or raw PGN game notation directly into your Student Dashboard portal. Our cloud Stockfish 16 engine processes the moves within seconds, identifying tactical blunders, missed forced mates, and key turning points with personalized commentary from titled Grandmasters.
FIDE RATINGS

Are academy tournament games official FIDE rated?

Yes! All Classical and Rapid tournament events hosted on our platform are officiated by certified International FIDE Arbiters. Ratings earned during these events are registered directly with FIDE and update your official international FIDE profile.
BATCH TRANSFERS

Can I switch training batches or reschedule GM sessions?

Absolutely. If your schedule changes or your ELO rating increases significantly during a term, you can request a batch transfer or reschedule your 1-on-1 Grandmaster mentorship session up to 24 hours in advance through the Student Portal or Enrollment Desk.
TITLE MENTORSHIP

How does the Grandmaster Title Mentorship work?

Our Title Mentorship program pairs players rated 2000+ ELO directly with an assigned FIDE Senior Trainer or Grandmaster. The curriculum includes customized opening novelties, psychological match preparation, and intensive endgame tablebase drills.
