Progressive Roadmap

The Master's Curriculum

Systematic four-stage advancement designed to elevate your rating at every benchmark.

Stage 1: Tactical Foundation (0 - 1200 ELO)
Pins, forks, skewers, discovered checks, basic mating nets, and eliminating piece blunders under time controls.
Stage 2: Positional Strategy (1200 - 1600 ELO)
Pawn structures, outposts, open files, space advantages, and piece coordination across closed centers.
Stage 3: Master Class (1600 - 2000 ELO)
Prophylaxis, exchanging pieces with purpose, dynamic imbalance play, and complex pawn sacrifices.
Stage 4: Title Preparation (2000+ ELO)
Tailored Grandmaster preparation, psychological match preparation, and deep computer-assisted novelties.
